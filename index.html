Tickets ready at the door

Drop in the PDF you received. In The Pit reads the artist, date, venue and price for you. At the entrance, one tap opens a black screen with only the QR code (or the barcode), big and sharp. It is prepared in advance, so it shows instantly, even with no signal at the venue. The PDF stays one icon away for the staff.

  • Paper ticket? Photograph it. It is straightened, cropped and stored as a very light image.
  • Add a note to the photo. Friends read it translated into their own language.
  • Venue membership cards go in too, with their own QR screen one tap away from your ticket.

Find any concert in seconds

Switches for your concerts, your friends’ concerts, the public list, past, upcoming, opening acts and not seen yet. Sort by date either way, and combine two keywords with and / or.

  • A slider keeps only what is within reach of home, and the distance shows on every concert.
  • Your filters are remembered, and one blue button resets them all.
  • The search also finds friends, by name or email.

Concerts are better with friends

Share your list by email. Your friends’ concerts appear in yours, tagged with their name, and you can rename them however you like. You get a notification when a friend adds a concert.

  • Your venue memberships stay private. Only photos and notes of old tickets are shown to your friends.

Questions

Do I need to install anything?

No. Open it in your browser. If you like, add it to your home screen from the browser menu and it behaves like an app.

Who can see my concerts?

You, and the friends you share with. You can also choose to appear, anonymously, in the public guest list. It is off by default and shows no name, notes, prices or tickets.

What does it do with my tickets?

An AI reads PDF tickets to fill in artist, date, venue and price. It is told to ignore your name, address and barcodes. The QR code or barcode itself is read on your phone, not by the AI. Paper tickets are kept as photos, and you decide what to add.
